Solver: local demo (in your browser)
How the solver worksRuns a local exact-search demo solver in your browser unless QpiAI Cloud is connected above. When connected, it POSTs JSON {Q, constant, hold_options_s, buses} to your endpoint with a Bearer key and expects back an array of chosen hold-option indexes (or {choices:[…]}); if the call fails it falls back to the local solver. You can also define window.QPIAI_SOLVER(problem) to send problem.Q to them and return the chosen hold option index for each bus.
